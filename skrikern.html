Skriker`n

Skriker`n is Savo Sound’s circuit-inspired emulation of the classic TS808 Tube Screamer.

Rather than treating the original pedal as a generic distortion curve, Skriker`n models the behavior of the TS808 signal path — from the input stage through the characteristic JRC4558D-based gain and diode-feedback clipping circuit, tone section, level control and output stage.

The result is the familiar mid-focused overdrive, soft clipping and dynamic response associated with the original hardware, with the Drive, Tone and Level controls designed to behave naturally across their full range.

For additional flexibility in a DAW, clean ±24 dB input and output controls are provided outside the modeled pedal circuit. This lets you control how hard the virtual pedal is driven without changing the behavior of the circuit itself.
