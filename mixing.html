INFO AND INQUIRIES

Mixing

A mix should sound like the record you intended to make.

Savo Sound offers mixing for artists and producers who want to bring their production together without losing what made it theirs in the first place.

Every mix is approached individually. There is no fixed chain, preset or template that gets dropped onto every song. The decisions are made around the recording, the production and where the artist wants the music to go.

The approach is simple: keep what already works, fix what doesn't, and bring out the clarity, depth, balance and impact the production needs.

Mixing at Savo Sound is handled by Espen N. Krogstad, who has worked with recording, mixing and music production since 2010 and studied sound and music production at Noroff.

Mixing only — mastering is not included.

Before You Send Your Project

Please send consolidated audio files starting from the same point in the song, with all files clearly named.

24-bit / 48 kHz WAV files are preferred. If your project is already recorded at a different sample rate, there is no need to convert it solely for delivery — just send the original files.

Include your rough mix if you have one. It doesn't have to sound polished — it gives me a useful reference for the balance, effects and direction you've been working toward.

You're also welcome to include reference tracks or a few notes about what you're looking for. They aren't there to be followed blindly, but to give a better understanding of where you want the record to go.

If processing or effects are an important part of the sound you've created, leave them in. If you're unsure whether something should be printed or removed, just include a note with the project.

What You Get

A finished Savo Sound mix is delivered as a complete mix package, not just a single stereo file.

MAIN MIX The finished stereo mix from the master bus, without final limiting, ready to be sent on for mastering.
LISTENING VERSION A separate version with limiting and final EQ correction, made for convenient listening and reference.
INSTRUMENTAL A complete instrumental version of the finished mix.
ACAPELLA The finished vocal mix without the instrumental.
STEREO STEMS A separate folder containing grouped stereo stems from the finished mix, giving you flexibility for mastering, alternate versions and future use.
FULL MIX TRACKS A separate folder containing the individual audio tracks that the finished mix is built from.

Everything is clearly organized and, unless otherwise agreed, delivered as 24-bit / 48 kHz WAV files.

Mastering is not included. The listening version is provided as a loudness- and playback-friendly reference and should not be considered a mastered version.
