VOJ

VOJ is Savo Sound's interpretation of the classic VOG-style bass resonance processor. It is designed to focus, strengthen and shape the low end by combining a powerful resonance with the filtering that happens below it.

Frequency chooses where the effect is centred, while Amplitude progressively increases the resonance and shapes the low end around it. Rather than simply adding a conventional EQ boost, the resonance and low-frequency roll-off work together as one response, allowing anything from subtle weight and focus to a much more pronounced low-end peak.

The 40, 100 and 200 modes cover different frequency ranges, while FLAT lets you bypass the resonant filtering without bypassing the plugin itself. The illuminated transformer switch engages an intentionally exaggerated analogue-inspired output stage, adding warmth, density, soft saturation and a more packed character.

VOJ is not a component-for-component emulation of the Little Labs VOG. It is an original Savo Sound implementation and interpretation of the concept, built around its characteristic approach to resonant low-frequency shaping.
