frEaQ

frEaQ is not designed to be just another corrective equalizer. It is built for shaping, coloring and giving character to sound.

While it works with the familiar controls and workflow of a parametric EQ, frEaQ responds to your boosts with more than simple level changes. The result is a more dimensional and characterful way of shaping the frequency spectrum.

Cuts remain clean and useful for conventional EQ work, while boosts are where frEaQ reveals its personality — adding weight, movement and character as you shape the sound.

Use it subtly to bring life to a source, or push it harder when you want the EQ itself to become part of the sound.
